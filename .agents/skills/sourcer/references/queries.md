# Mandatory 21-query statewide discovery pass

For each group, combine the canonical state name and common abbreviations with
the named authority, asset class, and file/service terms. Run targeted variants
across official government domains, general web search, and repositories. Log
the exact strings and negative results. Do not substitute one broad query for a
group.

1. State land acquisition gazettes, acquisition notices, and legal schedules.
2. Revocations, reallocations, excisions, restorations, and returned land.
3. Government layouts, schemes, residential/commercial estates, and phases.
4. Forest reserves, wildlife reserves, community forests, and game reserves.
5. National/state parks, conservation areas, green belts, and open spaces.
6. Wetlands, mangroves, shorelines, floodplains, waterways, dams, and buffers.
7. Tourism, resort, waterfront, leisure, cultural, civic, and stadium compounds.
8. Government plantations, farms, ranches, agricultural and irrigation schemes.
9. Road, rail, drainage, and transport rights-of-way or expansion corridors.
10. Airports, airstrips, ports, jetties, terminals, and associated acquired land.
11. Pipelines, transmission lines, substations, utilities, and easements.
12. Schools, universities, hospitals, public institutions, and institutional land.
13. Master plans, model cities, planning schemes, zoning, and development control.
14. Military, police, security, prisons, strategic and other federal property.
15. Cadastral blocks, allocation schedules, certificate lists, and GIS reservations.
16. Compensation, resettlement, relocation, enumeration, and livelihood schedules.
17. Court judgments, disputes, double allocations, encroachment, and demolitions.
18. Coordinate-bearing files: survey plans, beacon schedules, tables, maps, and
    gazettes across PDF, image, Word, spreadsheet, CSV/text, and archives.
19. Spatial services and downloads: ArcGIS REST/Hub, WFS, WMS, GeoServer, API,
    GeoJSON, KML/KMZ, SHP, GPKG, DXF, geodatabases, and catalogue attachments.
20. Government-derived repositories: budgets, procurement attachments, EIAs,
    ESMPs, feasibility studies, statistical reports, research data, and theses.
21. General media and historical archives for missing asset names, changes,
    instrument references, fraud/encroachment risks, and links to original files.

Within each group, prefer sources in this order: legal instrument; responsible
government authority; official data/service; government-derived repository;
licensed/open dataset; reputable secondary source; media discovery lead.
